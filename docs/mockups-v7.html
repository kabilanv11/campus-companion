Campus Companion v7

The v6 screens with the combined look you picked from the ten themes. Every screen is shown in light and dark. Nothing functional changed from v6.

What came from where

  • Light canvas from Paper, an off-white with a faint teal tint.
  • Dark canvas from Midnight and Ember: near-black with a faint violet undertone, with cards one step up.
  • Inversion from Spotlight: the class happening now or next is inverted. It is Spotlight's deep teal-green on the light page and pale teal on the dark one.
  • Light card highlight and depth from Editorial: the next meal is a pale teal card, and the highlighted blocks float on a soft shadow.
  • Needs you from Storybook: its own card with an inset hairline, bigger course dots, and urgent labels as pills.
  • Round buttons from Editorial and Storybook: everything tappable is a pill.
  • Button colours from Parchment: deep teal-green in light mode and aqua in dark mode. The active tab and the + button use the same colour.
  • Type from Notion: Inter throughout, a bold and compact greeting, sentence-case section labels, and a serif line under the greeting.
  • Next meal in dark mode is a deep teal card.
